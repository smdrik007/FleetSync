Tracking with limits.

Knowing where things are should not mean watching people. These are rules in the product, not promises on a slide.

  • Trip-bound GPS

    A driver is located only while a trip is running. Ending the trip ends tracking.

  • One institution, one boundary

    Every record belongs to your organisation. No other tenant's data can appear in your screens.

  • Roles decide access

    Authority, driver, passenger and guardian each see only the screens made for them.

  • Guardians see their people

    A guardian can follow exactly the people they are linked to, and nobody else.

Questions people ask first.

Do I need to buy tracking hardware?

No. A driver's phone sends its position through the browser while a trip is running. If you want dedicated trackers for people or equipment, you can register those devices in the console.

Can drivers be tracked when they are off duty?

No. Position is only collected between Start trip and End trip, and the driver sees a clear status while it is on.

Who can see my data?

Only people signed in to your institution. Every record is scoped to it, and each role sees only its own screens.

Can I track people or equipment, not just vehicles?

Yes. Choose People or Things when you sign up and you get trackers, safe zones, guardians and alerts instead of the fleet screens.

Do passengers need an account?

Yes. A passenger signs in to their institution to see live vehicles. You create those sign-ins from the Users screen.

What happens if a phone loses signal mid-trip?

Passengers see an amber Last seen badge with the time of the last position, so a stale dot never passes for a live one.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Your account and data stay. Tell us which plan you want and our team activates it, because online billing is not switched on yet.
